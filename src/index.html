Computing on data no one has to reveal.

OBLIV Lab — short for oblivious, the cryptographic property of computing without seeing — builds cryptographic protocols and systems that let organisations analyse, train on, and transact with sensitive data while it stays secret — even when a majority of participants are dishonest.

What we work on

Four directions, one principle: privacy by construction.

Secure multi-party computation

Efficient protocols for comparison, aggregation, and evaluation in the dishonest-majority model — the setting where most parties may collude.

Private AI and machine learning

Training and inference on data that never leaves its owner: private RLHF, privacy-preserving prediction, and secure activation functions.

Robust federated learning

Aggregation that survives malicious majorities, poisoning, and inference attacks — without a trusted server.

Secure and private payments

Anonymous task assignment, worker payment, and verifiable transactions for crowdsensing and digital finance.
Systems

Research that runs.

Each line of work ends in a working system with measured throughput and communication cost.

MPC

LTS+

Secure comparison for distributed data under a malicious majority. Two- to threefold throughput gain and up to 3× lower communication than prior work, with direct use in ReLU and federated aggregation.

Network security

Obliv-FW

Distributed, oblivious firewall built on Bloom filters and secret sharing. Rules are split across servers, so no single machine learns the policy, and updates need no downtime.

Verifiable systems

SVEvote

An e-voting design combining secret sharing, distributed hashing, and MAC verification to deliver ballot privacy, integrity, and public verifiability without central trust.

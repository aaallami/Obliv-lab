Join & collaborate

Work with us

We are a young lab with a clear agenda and an open door. Here is how different people usually get involved.

Researchers

Academic collaborators

We are seeking partners — particularly in the United States, Canada, and Australia — for joint papers, co-supervision of students, and joint funding proposals. We bring deep MPC expertise, fast protocol implementation, and a track record of student-led publications.

Email a short note about your project and what you'd like to build together.

Students

Prospective Ph.D. students

We currently have no funded positions, but Dr. Allami is open to supervising self-funded or scholarship-holding students, and to co-supervision arrangements with partner universities.

Strong background in algorithms, cryptography, or machine learning, and comfort writing code, is expected. Send a CV, transcript, and a paragraph on a paper of ours you found interesting.

Industry

Consulting and R&D

Dr. Allami is available for advisory and research-and-development engagements on privacy-preserving analytics, secure payments, federated learning, and confidential AI deployments.

Typical engagements: threat modelling, protocol design, prototype implementation, and independent review of privacy claims.
